# M07 — Portfolio & Case Studies

## Goal

Turn completed work and clearly-labelled internal concepts into credible, filterable case studies rather than a screenshot gallery.

## Depends on

M02 Data Platform, M04 Media & File Management, M05 Public Marketing Website, and service relationships from M06.

## Delivered

- `/work` portfolio index with shareable filters.
- `/work/[slug]` SEO-ready case-study template.
- `/admin/case-studies` CMS.
- `case_studies`, `case_study_services`, `case_study_media`, and `case_study_metrics`.
- Hero/gallery media integration with M04.
- Service-to-case-study relationships.
- Home page featured-work integration.
- M06 service page related-work resolution.
- Approved-only public metric/testimonial rules.
- Honest fallback/seed concepts with no fabricated client results.

## Acceptance notes

A real client metric or quote should not be entered as approved until publication permission and supporting context are confirmed.
